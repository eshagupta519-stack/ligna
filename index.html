    About Ligna

    Luxury, class and simplicity — in aluminium

    Our story

    Ligna was made to combine the purity and eco-friendliness of aluminium with expert craftsmanship, to create distinguished products for modern, minimalist interiors.

    Using state-of-the-art machinery, we produce aluminium systems in six finishes in elegant colours — wardrobes and their inner structures, sliding partitions, shelving, cubical systems, wall paneling, pole systems and handles. Each is designed to blend into its space while giving it order.

    Our designs embrace the trend of integrated living spaces, where one material language runs from room to room. The result is an interior that reflects the people who live in it — and lasts.

    Explore collectionsVisit the showroom

    • Purity of material

      Aluminium is light, strong and endlessly recyclable. We let it stay honest: slim profiles, clean joints, nothing superfluous.

    • Craftsmanship

      Precise machinery shapes every profile; skilled hands finish and fit it. The detail is where quality becomes visible.

    • Integrated living

      Systems that work together across a home or office — one language of finishes, proportions and hardware.
